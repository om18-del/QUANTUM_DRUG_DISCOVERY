# LIMITATIONS.md

What this build does **not** verify. Anything listed here is either unmeasured, unavailable, or known to be weaker than it looks; it must not be presented as a result.

## Known limitations

- **The quantum endpoints are a classical simulation, and the artifact cannot be regenerated here.** `GET /api/quantum/h2-curve` reads the stored grid; `POST /api/quantum/simulate` runs a real VQE loop (2-layer Ry ansatz, SciPy COBYLA, Qiskit estimator, seed-reproducible) graded against a NumPy diagonalisation of the same stored Pauli terms. It is still a local CPU simulation: no quantum hardware and no device-noise model. `noise` is accepted and echoed but never applied (shot-based runs do carry real sampling noise), and `within_chemical_accuracy` is deliberately `null` for shot-based runs — chemical accuracy is a noiseless claim only. A shot run's headline energy is a **fresh re-evaluation at the final optimal parameters** (not the minimum of the noisy optimisation trace) and carries its sampling uncertainty as `vqe_energy_std_ha`; the variational guard is scoped to noiseless statevector runs, so a shot run is never refused for sitting below exact on sampling noise. The 4-parameter ansatz's worst noiseless error over the whole grid is ~1e-3 Ha at the stretched 2.65–3.00 Å geometries (exact figures and command in RESULTS.md) — comfortably inside 1.6 mHa, but visibly not machine precision there. The artifact itself was generated offline with PySCF, which publishes no Windows wheel, so this build can validate it (`scripts/validate_hamiltonian_file.py`, `tests/test_h2_hamiltonian_artifact.py`) but cannot regenerate it locally.
- **Only H2 is simulated with VQE. LiH (active space) will serve only a precomputed curve and an exact reference once its artifact exists. Larger molecules are not simulated.** The curve endpoint takes `?molecule=` (`h2` default, `lih` known), and `GET /api/quantum/molecules` reports which artifacts exist. LiH's `lih_sto3g_active.json` is **not** in this repository: `available: false` and a `503` until the user generates it in Colab. The one ansatz in this build is a 2-qubit Ry+CX circuit, so a non-H2 `simulate` request is refused with `422 vqe_not_supported_for_molecule` rather than answered by an ansatz that was never written or tested, and the exact reference is refused above 8 qubits.
- **The resource estimate is counted from a formula; five of its orbital counts are machine-confirmed, the rest are not.** `GET /api/quantum/resources?formula=` (`POST /api/quantum/resources` is kept as a body-shaped alias) reports STO-3G spatial orbitals, electrons, full and reduced qubits, statevector memory, a feasibility verdict and an optional active-space block, with every figure tagged `exact` or `estimated`. `exact` there means closed-form in code — the counts follow the minimal-basis rule (H/He 1 function, Li–Ne 5, Na–Ar 9) and the neutral atomic numbers. **The orbital counts for H2, LiH, H2O, CH4 and C9H8O4 were cross-checked with PySCF `mol.nao` (STO-3G) in Colab and match** (2, 6, 7, 9, 73 spatial orbitals; 2, 4, 10, 10, 94 electrons); those five payloads carry `verified_against_pyscf: true`. **Every other formula is counted by the same rule but not machine-confirmed** and carries `false`, with the reason in `notes.verification`. No PySCF version is recorded, because none was captured, and neither the run nor a re-check happens in this repository (PySCF is not installed here). `active_orbitals` never changes the verification flag: it is an approximation layered on top of the full-molecule count. **No Pauli-term count and no circuit size is reported at all**, because neither follows from a formula; the only statement about term growth is the asymptotic "roughly N^4 in the number of orbitals". **The classical simulation limits are project choices and rough estimates** — an 8-qubit interactive cap and a rough 25-qubit laptop statevector ceiling, neither a measured hardware or algorithm bound — and `simulable_in_app` means the size fits this app's budget, **not** that a simulation exists for that molecule, because H2 is the only molecule with a VQE ansatz. Spin and charge are not modelled: the electron count is the neutral count from the formula and there is no charge input.
- **RDKit tests are skipped where RDKit is blocked.** On a host where the RDKit DLL is blocked, the RDKit-dependent tests skip instead of running. A skip is not a pass, and the conformer/property paths are unverified on that host.
- **The ligand N/O donor/acceptor rule is permissive.** Hydrogen-bond contact counts come from a geometric heuristic over deposited heavy atoms (no hydrogens, no protonation states), so they are **upper bounds**, not observed hydrogen bonds. The same applies to the salt-bridge rule. **Measured contacts are geometry, not a binding-affinity prediction**: a short contact says the two atoms are close in this crystal, not that the interaction contributes a given amount of binding energy. Nothing in the Studio is a docking score, a Kd, a Ki or an IC50.
- **Uncurated entries show geometry only.** `GET /api/complexes/explore/{pdb_id}` measures contacts for any deposited entry, but it has **no mechanism narrative, no catalytic-residue list and no literature source**: `curation` is `"none"`, the payload says "Not curated: measured contacts only", and the UI shows that badge with the mechanism and catalytic panels absent. Its numbers are the same geometric heuristics as everywhere else, with the same upper-bound caveat. The ligand rule (HETATM only, water/ions/sugars/cofactors/buffers/amino acids excluded, at least 10 heavy atoms) is a project rule, not a curated ligand assignment; an entry whose drug is deposited as a polymer chain cannot be explored at all (6LU7 is the example). The caps (25 MB of PDB text, 60,000 protein atoms) are project choices so a very large complex cannot hang the UI.
- **Most newly curated entries have no literature-verified residue list.** Only 2H42 has a residue (Gly659) named by the abstract that was fetched. For the others the payload carries `catalytic_residues_note` and the UI says the list is not curated, because writing catalytic residues from memory was explicitly not done. Mechanism statements are limited to what the fetched abstracts actually say; where an abstract gives no clinical effect, none is claimed (4EY7). The four original entries keep their original sources, which were not re-verified in this change.
- **CI has never run on a runner.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` exists, but no job has executed on a GitHub-hosted runner, so "tests pass in CI" is unverified.
- **The redocking pipeline exists but has never been run, and it cannot be run here.** `scripts/redock_validation.py` (SPEC.md P3-3, Part A) is the offline pipeline that would redock 1VRT, 2HU4 and 1HSG and measure each docked pose against the deposited crystal pose. AutoDock Vina and meeko publish no Windows wheel, so on this machine **no docking was performed, no pose was produced, and no RMSD exists**: `app/data/docking/redock_validation.json` is not in the repository, there are no redocking numbers in RESULTS.md, and nothing in the API or the UI reports one. What *was* verified here is everything that does not need Vina: the metadata-only ligand rule, the instance lookup, the chain selection and the receptor block were run against the three real RCSB entries, and the ligand preparation and the RMSD path were run against the real deposited coordinates using a synthetic pose (0.000 A at the crystal coordinates, exactly 3.000 A after a 3 A translation, and 0 A for a benzene ring written rotated by one position, where the atom-by-atom distance is 1.39 A). **The unverified code paths are receptor preparation, ligand PDBQT writing and the Vina call itself** — they are written against the documented meeko 0.8.0 and vina 1.2.7 APIs but no execution has checked them. The script fails a target rather than guessing when it cannot verify something at run time (an unreadable AutoDock type, a pose whose atoms do not match the ligand element by element, a receptor PDBQT with no hydrogens), and it checks before docking that the prepared ligand's own coordinates reproduce the crystal pose.
- **What a redocking pass would and would not show.** The success rule is the usual 2.0 A on the top-ranked pose. Passing it means the search rediscovered a pose **that was already inside a 22 A box centred on the true crystal site**, with the ligand's chemistry taken from the crystal: it is an easy test of a docking protocol, not evidence about affinity prediction, ranking, blind docking or virtual screening. A Vina score is a force-field **prediction, not measured binding** — it is not a Kd, Ki or IC50 and must never be compared with one. The three complexes are three complexes: nothing about the method, the force field or other targets follows from them, and one seed is one seed rather than a reproducibility claim. A target that fails to prepare or dock is recorded as `status: "failed"` with its reason and is never omitted, retried with other parameters or tuned into a pass; the file's `disclaimers` say all of this, and `scripts/validate_docking_file.py` refuses a file that drops one of those statements. **The first complete run at the fixed settings is the record, whatever it shows**: a rerun needs a dated log entry stating why, both results stay on file, and `tests/test_result_file_guardrails.py` validates the committed file automatically, so a result cannot reach the repository without the schema and honesty checks having run.
- **The receptor in 1VRT loses a modified residue, and no protonation states are assigned.** The receptor keeps only `ATOM` records of the chains within 5 A of the ligand, so waters, ions, the ligand and any modified residue deposited as `HETATM` are removed — 1VRT chain A has such a residue (CSD), and its atoms, backbone included, are absent from the receptor. Hydrogens are added by meeko at PDBQT preparation; no pH-based protonation assignment is performed, so the deposited protonation states are used as they are. Ligand alternate locations are handled by one documented rule (blank altLoc atoms plus the alphabetically first named altLoc), and the choice is recorded per target.
- **Part A does not touch the app.** The existing `GET /api/complexes/{id}/docking-validation` endpoint still serves curated placeholder data. Wiring a real result file into it is Part B, and until that happens no endpoint can report a redocking number.
